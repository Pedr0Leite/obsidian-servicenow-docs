## Issue

From time to time you might want to upload a file from your MID Server to your instance.

## Resolution

It is possible to use the MID Server to do so using the [ECC queue](https://docs.servicenow.com/csh?topicname=ecc-queue-mid-server.html&version=latest "ECC queue") however this is a customization. There have been many ServiceNow [Community](https://community.servicenow.com/community?id=community_article&sys_id=666c6ea1dbd0dbc01dcaf3231f96198a "Community") posts as well as our [Developer site](https://developer.servicenow.com/ "Developer site") that will help you to achieve this goal. Besides, you could contact your Salesperson or our Professional Services Team for additional guidance and support as each solution can differ from customer to customer.
