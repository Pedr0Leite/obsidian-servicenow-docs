## what are the roles required to Access Service Catalog

  

### Issue

What roles are required to access the Service catalog Module and how can you edit these.

### Release

All.

### Resolution

Below are the roles which are required to access Service catalog Module:  
  
 **Catalog, itil, Catalog\_manager, Catalog\_editor**  
  
To add or edit role for the Service Catalog Module. Please follow the steps below:  
\- Go to Filter Navigator.  
\- Type service catalog.  
\- Scroll on the Service catalog and you will see the pencil icon. (https://YourInstanceName.service-now.com/nav\_to.do?                       uri=%2Fsys\_app\_application.do%3Fsysparm\_clear\_stack%3Dtrue%26sys\_id%3Dd128ead2c0a8016501c6bbd3f9c03678)  
 -Here under roles section, you can manage different roles.
