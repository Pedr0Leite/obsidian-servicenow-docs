## Issue

Delegates who are not ITIL users are not receiving the approval emails. Each and every ServiceNow account involved is in good standing

## Resolution

Add them again as a delegate so they can continue to receive the approval emails and keep their delegate position.

To prevent this problem in the future, set up a reminder to change the dates before they expire.
