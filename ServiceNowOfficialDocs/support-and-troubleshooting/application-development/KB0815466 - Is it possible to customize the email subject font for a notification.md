## Is it possible to customize the email subject font for a notification

  

### Issue

Is it possible to highlight or bold part of the subject in a notification?

### Resolution

  
It is not possible to format Email Subject to bold, as the field is a simple text field and not a rich text or HTML field.  
The alternate option that we would suggest is to change the importance level marking it high / low per your requirement.
