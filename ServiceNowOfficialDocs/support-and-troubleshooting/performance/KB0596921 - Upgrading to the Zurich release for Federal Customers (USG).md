## Issue

At this time, Government Community Cloud (GCC) Customers are not eligible to participate in any upgrades to a Zurich version until FedRamp certification is received. It is not possible to schedule an upgrade to any Zurich version.

### Upgrading to the Zurich release in the future

The Zurich release has not been approved for Federal Customers (USG) because FEDRamp approval is not complete. When this release is approved for Federal Customers (USG), it will be available for scheduling (no longer blocked) in the Instances Dashboard portal on Now Support.   
  
If you have any questions, contact [USG Customer Support](/kb_view.do?sysparm_article=KB0547261 "USG Customer Support").

## Resolution

Customers can upgrade to Zurich after GA on 10th September 2025
