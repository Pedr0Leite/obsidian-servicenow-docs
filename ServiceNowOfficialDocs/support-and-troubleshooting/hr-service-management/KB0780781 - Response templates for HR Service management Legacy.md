## Issue

Is it possible  to work with Response Templates for legacy HR ?

## Resolution

Response templates are for Human Resources Scoped App: Core \[com.sn\_hr\_core\]

In order to use response templates Human Resources Scoped App: Core should be activated Ie HR case and knowledge management.

So, with legacy HR going by documentation, response templates cannot be used.
