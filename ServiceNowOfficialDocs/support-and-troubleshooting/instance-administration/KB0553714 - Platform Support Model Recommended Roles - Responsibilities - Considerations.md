## Platform Support Model: Recommended Roles - Responsibilities - Considerations

  

### Issue

ServiceNow recommends new customers to consider instantiating a ServiceNow Platform Support Team. This team serves a critical role in establishing, maintaining, and extending ServiceNow as a strategic business platform within the customer organization.

The responsibilities of the team ensure stability, performance, and a user experience that leads to high levels of satisfaction and includes:

-   Enhancement of existing application configurations
-   Development of new ServiceNow applications and services
-   Management and resolution of ServiceNow application incidents
