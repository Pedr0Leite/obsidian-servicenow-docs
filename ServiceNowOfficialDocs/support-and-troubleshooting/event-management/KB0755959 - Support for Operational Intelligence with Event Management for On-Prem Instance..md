## Issue

# Issue

Is Operational Intelligence with Event Management supported with an On-Prem instance.

# Explanation

We do not support Operational intelligence yet for on-Prem instances, we are currently testing the same and we will be supporting it soon. We do not have any specific time frame yet but the respective sales team should have more info once it is available.
