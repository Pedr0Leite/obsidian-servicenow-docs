## Whether discovery support for LDOMs (Logical Domains) on Solaris SPARC servers.

  

### Issue

Is their Discovery support for LDOMs (Logical Domains) on Solaris SPARC servers?

### Resolution

We do not currently support the discovery of LDOMs (Logical Domains) on Solaris SPARC servers OOB, but this is something that is being looked into as a future product enhancement.
