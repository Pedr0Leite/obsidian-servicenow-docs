## How to set time zones in Now Support

  

### Issue

Set your time zone in Now Support in two ways: in your user profile for general use, or when creating a case for case-specific communication. Your time zone setting helps customer support contact you at appropriate times. 

### Release

ServiceNow Support Instance

### Resolution

### Set your profile time zone

Set your time zone preference in your user profile. This setting determines the timestamps on items in Now Support and the default time zone for case communication. 

To set your time zone in Now Support:

1.  Log in to [Now Support](https://support.servicenow.com/now). 
2.  Select your name. 
3.  From the menu, select **My** **Profile**.
4.  In the Auto detect time zone for case communication field, select your time zone.
5.  Select **Update**.

 ![User profile page showing time zone settings](/sys_attachment.do?sys_id=2429efe493f57e188960fb2d6cba10c8 "User profile page showing time zone settings")

### Set a case-specific time zone  

ServiceNow customer support uses your profile time zone setting to determine the best time to contact you about cases.

If you do not have a time zone in your profile, you must select one when creating a case. You can also select a different time zone for a specific case if your profile time zone does not reflect your current availability. When you do this, you have the option to update your profile time zone at the same time.

To create a case with a case-specific time zone:

1.  Log in to [Now Support](https://support.servicenow.com/now). 
2.  Go to **My Support > Create a case**
3.  Complete the case form. In the final step, the **Case Communication Time Zone** field displays your profile time zone.
4.  If this time zone reflects your availability, continue to step 6. 
5.  If you need a different time zone for this case, select your preferred time zone from the **Case Communication Time Zone** list. A checkbox appears asking if you want to update your profile. Select the checkbox to update your profile time zone. 
6.  Complete the rest of the fields and select **Submit**.
