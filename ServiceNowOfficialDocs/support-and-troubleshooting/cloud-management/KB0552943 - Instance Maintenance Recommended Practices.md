## Issue

_Instance maintenance_ refers to the activities that a ServiceNow administrator can perform to monitor and maintain the overall health of their ServiceNow system.

Instance maintenance is often overlooked because the ServiceNow Platform is quite flexible and performs well under heavy demand. That said, with considered planning and periodic maintenance activities, you can ensure that your instance scales well and runs optimally at all times. It is worth pointing out that the ServiceNow Cloud Operations team has a comprehensive set of alerts and monitors, so both ServiceNow and your system administrators will always be notified if an instance is in a critical state. Support engineers (and customers) are notified through an incident on Now Support and mitigating actions are recommended to provide relief.

In our Customer Success Center you will find recommendations and good practices to help you proactively prevent your instances from getting into a critical state in the first place. Please keep in mind that this is not an exhaustive list of preventative activities and some items offer more benefit than others based on your specific implementation.

Look for more information in the our Customer Success Center's [ServiceNow instance performance and maintenance](https://www.servicenow.com/success/playbook/instance-performance-maintenance.html "ServiceNow instance performance and maintenance").
