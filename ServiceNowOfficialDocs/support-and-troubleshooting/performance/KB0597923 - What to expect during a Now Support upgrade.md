## Issue

Now Support is being upgraded to a new version of the ServiceNow applications. During the upgrade, you might experience temporary delays or performance changes as described in this article.

 **Note:** There are no impacts to functions such as submitting an incident or case.

## Resolution

### Impact on instance upgrades

-   There may be a delay in your upgrade starting
-   Updates to your upgrade change records may be delayed by up to two hours. As a result, your upgrade may be completed before you receive the closed change notification from Now Support  
    -   As always, you can set up your own upgrade completed notification within your instance and check for upgrade completion within your instance

For more information on upgrades, including the process and how to prepare, see [Upgrading your instance](https://docs.servicenow.com/csh?topicname=upgrades-overview.html&version=latest "Upgrading your instance").

### Issues with overall Now Support performance

During this version upgrade, you may experience slower performance on transactions and reports. Performance will return to normal at the completion of the upgrade.

If you experience a service outage or new issues, please [submit a case to ServiceNow Technical Support](https://support.servicenow.com/kb?id=kb_article_view&sysparm_article=KB0547260 "submit a case to ServiceNow Technical Support").
