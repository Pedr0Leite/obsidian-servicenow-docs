## \[SAMP Workspace\] How to add fields on the CI form within the Workspace

  

### Issue

How to add fields on the CI (configuration item) form within the Workspace

### Cause

There are views defined for Workspace and you will need to add the columns as per need.

### Resolution

1.  You can open the same CI on the legacy form view
2.  Now, right click on form header and View and then select the "CMDB Workspace" view. (Attached the screenshot)![](/sys_attachment.do?sys_id=a941409f93af8e50def533527cba1051)
3.  Now again right click on form header and select Configure and then form Layout  
    **\*\*** Here you may be asked to select the application scope. Since its in same scope you can choose "Edit this section"  
    4\. Here you can update the fields that you would like to see and then save.  
    5\. Now refresh the page and you will see the results.
