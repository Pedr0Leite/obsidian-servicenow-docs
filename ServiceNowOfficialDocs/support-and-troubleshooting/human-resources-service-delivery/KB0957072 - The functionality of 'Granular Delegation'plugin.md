## The functionality of 'Granular Delegation'plugin

  

### Summary

The plugin - com.glide.granular\_service\_delegation is new Quebec release.

  

Granular Delegation plugin will provide more flexibility in assigning delegation of records on behalf of users.  
  
Current delegation delegates all tasks and does not provide any indication of delegation.  
  
  
Granular Delegation allows the delegation specific tasks to be delegated to specific users at specific times. The delegate users will be informed when delegated task is assigned to them.
