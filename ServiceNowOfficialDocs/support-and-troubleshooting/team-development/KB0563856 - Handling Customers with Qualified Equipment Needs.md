## Issue

### Qualified instances and IQOQ customers

Customers on qualified instances are generally in a Life Science or other regulated space. They are required to receive documentation that the server/instance was checked and verified to be working as intended on set-up or major change. ServiceNow follows a process to set up the equipment and produces formal documentation when requested.

### Roles

Roles who need this information:

_Partners_ 

-   Need to ensure customers know that they must REQUEST qualified instances in accordance with the Customer Request for Qualified Instances and Servers Policy 
    
-   Make new equipment requests (for example, new instances) requiring IQOQ equipment via sales account management to ensure the contract noted properly)
    
-   Ensure work with existing customers on IQOQ equipment is treated with proper handling- indicate on tickets
    

_Customer Support_

-   May work an incident with a customer on the qualified instance
    
-   May receive requests for qualification or re-qualification of the instance 
    

_Sales_

-   May have customers who are on qualified instances and have issues or questions
    
-   Need to ensure customers know they must REQUEST qualified instances in accordance with the Customer Request for Qualified Instances and Servers Policy 
    

### IQOQ qualification importance and managing IQOQ customers

Qualification documentation is very important to our customers. The documentation serves as proof that we performed the checks on their equipment/instance and provided them with documentation. Customers use the documentation. Also, it could be required by their auditors as evidence of their due diligence with ServiceNow as a vendor storing their data. It is a "big deal" that they entrust this to us. It is important that it be done properly and when required.

If a customer requests a qualification, a sales account executive should be notified. Order management opens a change ticket on their behalf for this purpose/fulfilment. Please contact the customer's ServiceNow account executive to ensure proper documentation and processing.

If an IQOQ customer with existing qualified equipment has a problem or upgrade, we need to be sensitive that the changes could require re-qualification. This involves not only ServiceNow (who can only qualify the instance "out of the box,"  but customers must then "OQ" (Operational Qualification) for their use. This could involve a testing process that cannot be done quickly. Sometimes it is unavoidable, but customers want to know we recognize this and do not recommend fixes without considering the impact on them. They would want to do it in a calculated way.

### New requests

All new instance requests for customers saying they need qualified equipment must go through their sales account management. This ensures that their contract with ServiceNow is properly noted and the qualification processes in an accurate and timely manner.

**SLAs on new requests**

-   A member of the **Performance Engineering / System Administrator** team will respond to and schedule all requests within 15 days.
-   Starting on the first scheduled day, there is a 30-day period reserved for completing the request and the required documentation. If the request is completed earlier, documentation will be provided to the customer liaison at that time.
-   The qualification is not considered complete until the customer receives the documentation bundle. Documentation will be provided by the Corporate Quality System Management (QMS) Compliance team. Do not pull documentation directly from the change request record and provide it to customers, as sensitive and internal information is included.

### Managing current IQOQ customers

Sometimes changes made will prompt a need to re-qualify equipment. Discuss the change with the customer. If they feel it requires re-qualification, plan the timeline around processing by ServiceNow.  

### Escalations

If there are serious issues and management should be notified, request that support escalate following their standard escalation process.
