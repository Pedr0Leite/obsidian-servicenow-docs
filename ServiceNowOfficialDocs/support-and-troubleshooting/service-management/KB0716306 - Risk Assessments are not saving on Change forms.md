## Risk Assessments are not saving on Change forms

  

### Issue

After filling out the Risk assessment, looking at the Risk assessment tab, user can see it has created an assessment instance, but there is information that is missing such as "Completed Date" and "Completed by"

### Release

London Patch 1 HF 2

### Cause

Customized "Fill out Risk Assessment" UI Action

### Resolution

Revert the "Fill out Risk Assessment" UI Action to Out of Box
