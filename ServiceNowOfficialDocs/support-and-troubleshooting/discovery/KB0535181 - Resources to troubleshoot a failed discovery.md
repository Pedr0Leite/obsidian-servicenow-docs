## Resources to troubleshoot a failed discovery

  

### Issue

Discovery can fail due to issues such as incorrect network or device configurations. Check out the following articles for steps on how to troubleshoot a failed discovery with probes and sensors (not patterns).

### Symptoms

Discovery can fail for several reasons including:

-   No device found
-   Too many devices found
-   The wrong device is matched
-   The CMDB is not populated
-   Device lacks the necessary information
-   Duplicate records appear
-   Not enough records are present
-   Discovery stops

### Release

All

### Resolution

If any of the following issues apply to your environment, review the related knowledge article to help address and resolve potential issues.

Discovery fails during the following phases:

1.  **Are you there** phase.  
    -   See [Troubleshooting the Shazzam Phase in Discovery](/kb?id=kb_article_view&sysparm_article=KB0535234 "KB0535234: Shazzam Phase Troubleshooting")  
          
        
2.  **What are you** phase.  
    -   See [Troubleshooting the Classification Phase in Discovery](/kb?id=kb_article_view&sysparm_article=KB0535236 "KB0535236: Classification Phase Troubleshooting")  
          
        
3.  **Have I seen you before** phase.  
    -   See [Troubleshooting the Identification Phase in Discovery](/kb?id=kb_article_view&sysparm_article=KB0535238 "KB0535238: Identification Troubleshooting")  
          
        
4.  **What else can you tell me about yourself** phase.  
    -   See [Troubleshooting the Exploration Phase in Discovery](/kb?id=kb_article_view&sysparm_article=KB0535240 "KB0535240: Exploration Troubleshooting")

**Note**: If your problem still exists after trying these steps, submit an incident to customer support and note this knowledge base article ID (KB0535181) in the problem description.
