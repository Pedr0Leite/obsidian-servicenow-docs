## Issue

The ServiceNow service interruption page is served to clients, by our load balancer, due to either:

-   No available application pool members (Unplanned Outage)
-   An ongoing scheduled maintenance (Planned Outage)

## Resolution

Before raising a Hi case with ServiceNow Support, please check for possible scheduled maintenance activities, planned in the same timeframe.

We raise customer facing changes for scheduled maintenance activities, that require downtime.

These changes have the Primary and Secondary Support contacts, for your Company (as recorded in Hi), in the Watchlist.

The Primary and Secondary Support contacts can be changed by you, in the Hi Portal.

* * *

If service is not restored within the planned maintenance timeframe, please reach out to ServiceNow Support.
