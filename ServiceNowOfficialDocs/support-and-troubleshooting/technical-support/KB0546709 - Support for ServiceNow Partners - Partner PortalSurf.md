## Issue

This article provides instructions regarding support for ServiceNow Partners.

### Procedure

For support issues and requests, please review the following:

-   **Partner portal issues/questions NOT related to Partner portal access;**
-   **Partner program issues/questions on the partner dashboard;**
-   **Deployment registration issues and access to end customer accounts or partner CSAT;**
-   **SURF login issues;**
-   **SURF requests, questions, and issues:**  
      
        ↳ Submit a case through the [Partner Portal's "Support" module.](https://partnerportal.service-now.com/partnerhome?id=submit_inquiry)  
        ↳ Our Partner Portal team will route the case to the appropriate team.  
      
    
-   **Partner portal access issues should be directed to:** [partnernow@servicenow.com](mailto:partnernow@servicenow.com "partnernow@servicenow.com")
-   **Partner Team North America:**  833.295.3612
