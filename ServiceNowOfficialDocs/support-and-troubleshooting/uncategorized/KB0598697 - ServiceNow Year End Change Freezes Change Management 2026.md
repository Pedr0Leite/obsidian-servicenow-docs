## Issue

This article provides the details for Change Freezes in 2026, including North America Thanksgiving and Global Year-End. At ServiceNow, we recognize the importance of service availability, especially during the holidays.

To establish an elevated level of precaution during this time, we will be implementing two-holiday Freezes on ServiceNow initiated Changes during North America's Thanksgiving, Christmas, and New Year holiday periods.

###   
Freeze Dates and Times

**Thanksgiving and Cyber Monday** (All North America Data Centers)

-   Monday, November 23, 2026, @ 0100 PT – Tuesday, December 01, 2026, @ 0100 PT

**Year End Change Freeze** (All Global Data Centers) 

-   Monday, December 14, 2026, @ 0100 PT – Saturday, January 2, 2027, @ 0100 PT

###   
Support Process

During the Change Freeze periods, our support processes will operate as normal. ServiceNow will not be scheduling any service impacting maintenance to take place within the designated Change Freeze dates. However, should the need arise, emergency maintenance will be approved during this period.

If you have any questions please contact the ServiceNow Technical Support team by visiting the [Contact Us](https://support.servicenow.com/kb?id=kb_article_view&sysparm_article=KB1000420 "Contact Us") page for contact information for your region.

###   
Escalation

If expert assistance is needed with performing any procedure or further information on this document, please contact the ServiceNow Technical Support team by visiting the [Contact Us](https://support.servicenow.com/kb?id=kb_article_view&sysparm_article=KB1000420 "Contact Us") page for contact information for your region.

###   
FAQs

**What does this mean for our customers?  
**ServiceNow will not be implementing any service that impacts customer created changes during the Freeze period. Customers can continue to initiate changes on their own instances. Business will be as usual for customers and partners.  
  

**Can customers still plan upgrades during the Freeze period?**  
Yes, customers can still plan upgrades via the Service Catalog on NowSupport.
