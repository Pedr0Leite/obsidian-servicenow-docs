Favorites Overview

* * *

Favorites are a quick way to access frequently used Now Support (HI) pages and records. Favorites can be accessed via the Favorites tab in the left navigation. They are also visible when left navigation is collapsed.

You can add favorites for links to the left navigation in Now Support (HI) for:

-   Now Support (HI) pages
-   Incidents
-   Change Requests
-   Problems
-   Service Catalog
-   and more…

Adding Favorites

* * *

**Adding a page to favorite**

1.  Navigate in Now Support (HI) to find the page to add as a favorite.
2.  Click the **Favorites** icon.

![](sys_attachment.do?sys_id=030964aedb02b450e515c2230596199b)

The page marked as favorite shows up in left navigation under **FAVORITES**

![](sys_attachment.do?sys_id=0f0964aedb02b450e515c223059619a0)

**Adding a record to favorite**

1.  Navigate in Now Support (HI) to find the record to add as a favorite.
2.  Click the **Favorites** icon.

![](sys_attachment.do?sys_id=c70964aedb02b450e515c223059619a6)

Managing Favorites

* * *

1.  Click the **Favorites** tab in the left navigation to open it.
2.  Click **Manage Favorites**
3.  A dialog appears providing users the following options:
    1.  Rearrange favorites
    2.  Delete favorites 
4.  The pages or records favorited can be rearranged by dragging and dropping using ![](sys_attachment.do?sys_id=530964aedb02b450e515c223059619d6). This affects their order of appearance in the Favorites menu.
