### Issue

# Netscaler Content Switching Discovery / Service Mapping

* * *

NetScaler load balancer has patterns from horizontal and top down discovery.

Our OOB NetScaler pattern doesn't have any specific support for content switching as content switching can be set different in each customer device. 

# Additional Information

* * *

-   Advise the customer that Netscaler Load Balancer with Content Switching is enabled is not supported in OOB. 
-   Consult ServiceNow Professional Services or other partner to implement the solution.
-   Or, they may consider to raise an Enhancement Request.
