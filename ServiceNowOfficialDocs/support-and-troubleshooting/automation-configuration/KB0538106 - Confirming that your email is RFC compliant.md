## Confirming that your email is RFC compliant

  

### Issue

Confirming that your email is RFC compliant  

Problem

* * *

Emails with attachments cannot be sent or received.  

Symptoms

* * *

Symptoms may include the following:  

-   Attachment does not reach instance
-   Attachment not received
-   Attachment not associated to record
-   Attachments not processing
-   Inbound email not adding attachments
-   Cannot email an attachment
-   Too many attachments (signature)
-   Email contains attachment named winmail.dat
-   Cannot open attachment
-   Attachment size limit

  
Resolution

* * *

Confirm that your email is RFC compliant. ServiceNow follows RFC 2822 (Internet Message Format). For more information visit [RFC 2822](http://www.rfc-editor.org/info/rfc2822 "RFC 2822"). If your email message follows the rules from RFC 2822 and is not processed correctly by the ServiceNow instance, then you are encouraged to submit an incident.
