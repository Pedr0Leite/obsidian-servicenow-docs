## Planning Console not loading, console error: Unkown Provider (PWGanttUtilsProvider)

  

### Issue

# Symptoms

* * *

Planning tab of the planning console is not loading. Error in the browser console: Unknown provider: PwGanttUtilsProvider -> PwGanttUtils

# Release

* * *

Kingston Patch 8

# Cause

* * *

Customized Planning Console service portal widget.

# Resolution

* * *

Revert to the out of box planning console widget.
