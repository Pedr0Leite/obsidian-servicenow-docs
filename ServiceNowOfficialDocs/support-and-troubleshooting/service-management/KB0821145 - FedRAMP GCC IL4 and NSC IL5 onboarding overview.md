## Overview

This article provides a high-level overview of the onboarding process for ServiceNow Government Community Cloud (GCC) and National Security Cloud (NSC) environments. Review the end-to-end process, identify responsibilities, and find detailed instructions for each step. For a visual representation of the process, see the image at the end of this article. 

For complete details on onboarding steps, see [How to complete DISA BCAP onboarding for GCC or NSC.](/kb?id=kb_article_view&sysparm_article=KB0819715)

## Process phases

Complete the following phases to onboard to ServiceNow FedRAMP GCC (IL4) or NSC (IL5) environments.

### **Phase 1: FedRAMP High IL4 discussions and eligibility criteria** 

**Responsibility:** Customer and ServiceNow

Verify that your organization meets the eligibility and qualification requirements for GCC or NSC.

**Reference documentation:** For eligibility criteria, see [ServiceNow GCC FedRAMP / IL4 Cloud Offering.](/kb?id=kb_article_view&sysparm_article=KB1120494 "ServiceNow GCC FedRAMP / IL4 Cloud Offering")

### **Phase 2: Establish portal access**

**Responsibility**: Customer

Create your portal account and configure PIV or CAC authentication. MFA, CAC, or PIV authentication is required.

**Reference documentation:** For instructions, see [How to access GCC or NSC with PIV or CAC authentication](/kb?id=kb_article_view&sys_kb_id=d6516f869331f290c2513f986cba1008). 

### **Phase 3: Complete DISA BCAP onboarding process**

**Responsibility:** Customer

Complete the seven-step DISA Boundary Cloud Access Point (BCAP) onboarding process, including IATT, SNAP registration, CPTC, SCCA, and PPSM requirements.

**Reference documentation:** For instructions, see [How to complete DISA BCAP onboarding for GCC or NSC.](/kb?id=kb_article_view&sysparm_article=KB0819715)

### **Phase 4: Custom URLs and DNS registration** 

**Responsibility:** Customer and ServiceNow 

Configure a custom URL for your GCC or NSC instance. This phase is optional and customer-driven.

**Reference documentation:** For instructions, see [Configure a custom URL](/kb?id=kb_article_view&sysparm_article=KB1273007). 

### **Phase 5: Cyber Security Service Provider (CSSP) setup**

**Responsibility:** Customer and ServiceNow 

Configure CSSP services for your environment. This phase is optional and customer-driven.

### **Ongoing: Compliance monitoring and documentation**

**Responsibility:** Customer and ServiceNow

Access compliance authorization packages, security documentation, and FedRAMP continuous monitoring information.

**Reference documentation:** For compliance documentation, see [US Federal Compliance Authorization Package Access](/kb?id=kb_article_view&sys_kb_id=6959384447f74690c4e1a325126d43c6).
