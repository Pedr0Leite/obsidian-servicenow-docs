## How to view your cases, changes, and problems on Now Support

  

### Issue

You can view information about your issues from the [Now Support portal](/kb_view.do?sysparm_article=KB0547103 "Now Support portal"). Issues include cases, problems, changes, and enhancement requests that you and others in your company create. 

If you're a ServiceNow partner, you can also view cases for your company and the customers you manage.

**Note**: Your available options depend on your Now Support account roles. Contact your ServiceNow admin if you can't access a feature.

### Release

  All releases

### Resolution

#### For customers and partners

1.  Go to [Now Support](https://support.servicenow.com/now "Now Support")
2.  On the home page, you can view recent case activities and upcoming changes. This dashboard lists cases, changes, and problems. The default view shows all issues for you, with an option to switch to the company view.   
      
    ![My Support Home Page](/sys_attachment.do?sys_id=408fc98f47217250c2488d01426d4356)  
      
      
    ![](/sys_attachment.do?sys_id=b770558347617250c2488d01426d43bf)  
      
    ![](/sys_attachment.do?sys_id=b29019c347617250c2488d01426d4325)  
      
      
    
3.  Select **View all** to open the list view. You can use this view to:
    -   Search for a specific record.
    -   Apply filters.
    -   Sort records by selecting a column header.
    -   Export records in PDF, Excel, or CSV format.  
          
        ![](/sys_attachment.do?sys_id=d71e058747217250c2488d01426d43e3)
4.  Select a record number to open the record. You can view details and the activity stream to check progress. You can also add comments, respond to questions from ServiceNow Support, add attachments, or update the watchlist.

  
![](/sys_attachment.do?sys_id=531e058747217250c2488d01426d43b5)

#### For partners who manage customer company data: 

You must have the partner role to perform the following steps.

1.  Navigate to [Now Support](https://support.servicenow.com/now) and log in.
2.  Select your user name and select **Switch Company**. A list of companies you can manage appears.   
      
    ![](/sys_attachment.do?sys_id=a71e458747217250c2488d01426d430d)  
      
      
      
    ![](/sys_attachment.do?sys_id=ab1e458747217250c2488d01426d4311)  
      
    
3.  Select a company. This updates multiple portal pages with the customer company data, including home page widgets, instance pages, accounts, and issues.
