## Azure Pipelines fails to connect to ServiceNow and throws error "App is not compatible"

  

### Issue

Azure Pipelines fails to connect to ServiceNow showing the error message stating 'App is not Compatible'

### Cause

This is a store app developed by the vendor 'Microsoft Alliance' and not ServiceNow. To identify why it complains that the app is not compatible is not something ServiceNow support can assist with. 

### Resolution

You would need to contact the vendor for support.
