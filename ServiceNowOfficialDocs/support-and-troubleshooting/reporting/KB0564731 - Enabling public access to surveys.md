## Enabling public access to surveys

  

### Issue

Enabling public access to surveys 

Overview

* * *

Starting with the Geneva release, the Survey functionality supports public surveys.

This feature can be used to let people take surveys without logging in. This can be very useful for capturing general public opinions, feedback, or market research.

Making a survey publicly available

* * *

A Survey admin can make a survey public by clicking the **Enable Public Access** ui action on the Survey definition UI. The **View survey URL** can be used to view and get and view the survey URL.
