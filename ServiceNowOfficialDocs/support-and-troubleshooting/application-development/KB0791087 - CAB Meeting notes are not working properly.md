## CAB Meeting notes are not working properly

  

### Issue

Notes made in the CAB meeting are not updating the meeting record properly and other users are unable to see the notes made by the manager. The save button on the meeting will constantly blink in and out.

### Release

London

### Cause

The issue is with the Network. As a ServiceNow employee, I was unable to reproduce the issue and the caller was unable to reproduce it off their companies network.

### Resolution

Reach out to your internal network team and have them troubleshoot your Network configurations.
