## Altering Agenda Management "Change Requests Starting after" and "Change Requests starting on or before" for every CAB meeting

  

### Issue

It is possible to modify CAB dates on individual meetings and to create a new meeting with multiple weeks on the agenda. CAB definitions are similar to a recurring meeting invitation. When you define a CAB definition, it determines the attributes that are associated with all CAB meetings generated from that definition.
