## Software Asset Managment and SaaS License filter navigator modules are missing

  

### Issue

Software Asset Managment and SaaS License filter navigator modules are missing after in the installation of the Software Asset Workspace.

### Release

This is not release specific.

### Cause

After the installation of Software Asset Workspace, the SaaS License filter navigator modules are deactivated.

### Resolution

We recommend the use of the Software Asset Workspace, which is the intended experience and includes additional modules for convenience. If the out of the box behaviour does not meet your requirements, you may choose to enable the filter navigator modules.
