## Issue

Employee Documents not getting purged, even after the Purge date is completed. HR Criteria and the retention period of the employee document are all correctly configured.

## Resolution

Once the user details were updated in the scheduled job to an active user, we see the scheduled job ran and deleted the documents as expected.
