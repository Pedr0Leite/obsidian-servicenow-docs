## Issue

This article describes the procedure to limit Cloud discovery on specific Logical Datacenters only

## Resolution

 You can limit the discovery schedule to a specific datacenter region. In order to do that, follow the below steps :  
  
1) Navigate to the service account  
  
2) Go to the related tab Discovery - Config Logical datacenters  
  
3) Open the record and attach the specific datacenter in the logical data center field  
  
This will make sure the discovery schedule runs only on the specified datacenter in that field.
