## Issue

NowSupport Administrators are Now Support users with the **NS admin** role. They create and manage users on Now Support at [https://support.servicenow.com/now](https://support.servicenow.com/now "https://support.servicenow.com/now"). ServiceNow requires all customers to have at least one Customer Administrator at all times.

Customers can [identify their NowSupport administrator](/kb_view.do?sysparm_article=KB0547253 "Identifying Customer Administrator") by navigating to their user profile in Now Support. If you are having trouble logging in to Now Support and do not know who your NowSupport Administrator is, please contact [Technical Support](https://support.servicenow.com/kb_view.do?sysparm_article=KB0547260 "Customer Support").

**Note**: To request an account on Now Support, please contact your NowSupport Administrator or speak to your ServiceNow territory manager. If you are not sure who that is, please contact [Technical Support](https://support.servicenow.com/kb_view.do?sysparm_article=KB0547260 "Customer Support").

### Roles and Responsibilities

NowSupport Administrators are responsible for:

-   [Managing the user list](https://support.servicenow.com/kb_view.do?sysparm_article=KB0547279 "Managing the user list")

-   Managing access to Now Support  
    -   [Contacting Technical Support](/kb_view.do?sysparm_article=KB0547260 "Customer Support")
    -   Unlocking users
    -   [Deactivating and activating users](/kb_view.do?sysparm_article=KB0547277 "Activating and deactivating users")
    -   Updating Roles for users

-   Managing company contacts  
    -   Keeping contact information [up to date](/kb_view.do?sysparm_article=KB0547262 "Customer Administration")
    -   [Security Contacts in Now Support](https://support.servicenow.com/kb_view.do?sysparm_article=KB0621516 "Security Contacts in Now Support")
-   Managing instance information  
    -   [Obtaining IP address and datacenter information](/kb_view.do?sysparm_article=KB0538621 "Obtaining IP address and datacenter info")
