## Managing my user information on Now Support

  

### Issue

Use the [Customer Self-Service Portal](/kb_view.do?sysparm_article=KB0547103 "Customer Self-Service Portal") on Now Support to manage your user information, including phone numbers, email address, and title. You can view, add, or update any details in your user information.

### Accessing and editing user information

1.  Navigate to [https://support.servicenow.com/now](https://support.servicenow.com/now "https://support.servicenow.com/now").
2.  Click your name in the upper right corner of the portal.  
      
    ![](/sys_attachment.do?sys_id=497bd13cdb01b410b55f0b55ca961979)  
      
    
3.  Select **My Profile**.  
      
    ![](/sys_attachment.do?sys_id=457bd13cdb01b410b55f0b55ca96197b)  
      
    
4.  Edit the information.  
    -   You cannot edit **Name** and **User ID**. To change your name or user ID, contact your [customer administrator](/kb_view.do?sysparm_article=KB0547256 "customer administrator").
    -   You can use this page to update the avatar icon or to change your Now Support password along with your contact numbers.
    -   You can also use this page to set your current geo location and time zone if needed.
    -   For more information about the fields, see [Creating and managing Now Support users](/kb_view.do?sysparm_article=KB0547279 "Creating and managing Now Support users").
