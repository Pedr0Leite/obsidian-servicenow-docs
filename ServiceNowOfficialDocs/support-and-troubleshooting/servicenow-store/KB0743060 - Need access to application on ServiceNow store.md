## Need access to application on ServiceNow store

  

### Issue

Federal Customer needs access to ServiceNow Store

[ServiceNow Store](https://store.servicenow.com/sn_appstore_store.do#!/store/home "ServiceNow Store")

### Release

All Supported

### Resolution

Client needs to contact the vendor directly.

Select the application and then on the right side it has the contact information on the GET.
