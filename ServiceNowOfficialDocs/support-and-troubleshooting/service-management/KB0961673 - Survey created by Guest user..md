## Survey created by Guest user.

  

### Issue

Seeing surveys generated by Guest user which were not assigned out.

### Cause

assessment\_take2 page set to public.

### Resolution

Survey definitions have a general survey URL provided in the 'View Survey URL' related link. When unauthenticated users click this link the system generates a survey for Guest user. Out of box the assessment\_take2 page is set to public in the Public (sys\_public) table. By setting the assessment\_take2 page to false in this table, it will prevent unauthenticated users from triggering general surveys.
