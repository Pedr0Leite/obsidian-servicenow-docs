## Employee Service Center (ESC) throwing multiple "The page you are looking for could not be found." / Page not found errors

  

### Issue

The issue occurs after activating the Employee Service Center (ESC) plugin for HR and the below error message appears

![](sys_attachment.do?sys_id=24aab801db84b4d0b55f0b55ca9619a4)

### Cause

Due to a customized header widget.

### Resolution

 To resolve the issue,

1.  Revert the widget to Out of Box (OOB),
2.  Check the ESC homepage now and it will be displayed properly.
