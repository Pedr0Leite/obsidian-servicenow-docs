## Issue

We do not see the software asset management plugins in plugins list and are not sure what is active on our instance.

## Resolution

In order to see what is active on your instance, you can navigate to the sys\_plugins.LIST 

If you would want to see a list of plugins and their status, you can also navigate to sys\_package.LIST and ensure to have the active column in the view.
