## Upgrade/Downgrade rights association when using Software asset management professional/premium

  

### Issue

# Overview

* * *

With Software asset management premium/professional plugin, the ability to associate an upgrade or downgrade license is available only on an entitlement level and not on the software model.

  

With just legacy software asset management plugin active, the **software model** records have an upgrade and a downgrade tab:

![](Screen%20Shot%202018-12-14%20at%203.51.26%20PM.pngx)![](sys_attachment.do?sys_id=deaaa8a6db42b450e515c22305961920)

  

With Software Asset Management Premium/Professional, the **software entitlements** tab have the upgrade/downgrade tabs:

![](sys_attachment.do?sys_id=2aaaa8a6db42b450e515c22305961926)

  

With Software Asset Management Premium/Professional, the **software model record do not** have the upgrade or downgrade tabs:

![](sys_attachment.do?sys_id=22aaa8a6db42b450e515c2230596192c)
