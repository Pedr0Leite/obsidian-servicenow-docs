## Document Viewer - Viewing any attachment fails with error "The Document cannot be previewed" after upgrade to NY

  

### Issue

Document Viewer - Viewing any attachment fails with error "The Document cannot be previewed" after upgrade to NY

### Cause

The issue is because of browser caching.

### Resolution

Clearing the users browser cache resolves the issue.
