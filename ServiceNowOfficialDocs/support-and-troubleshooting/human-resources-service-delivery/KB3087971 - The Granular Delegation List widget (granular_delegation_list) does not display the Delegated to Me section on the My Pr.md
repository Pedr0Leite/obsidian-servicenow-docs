## Issue

On the My Profile page, the Work Items tab uses the Granular Delegation List widget to show delegation records. You are expecting to see two sections:

-   My Delegates — people the logged-in user has delegated tasks to
-   Delegated to Me — people who have delegated tasks to the logged-in user

However, only the "My Delegates" section renders. The "Delegated to Me" section is absent.

## Resolution

This is a current platform limitation. There is no supported workaround to display the "Delegated to Me" section within the widget at this time.

This behavior is under review for a future release.In the meantime, we recommend using the To-Dos feature to keep track of tasks that have been delegated to you.
