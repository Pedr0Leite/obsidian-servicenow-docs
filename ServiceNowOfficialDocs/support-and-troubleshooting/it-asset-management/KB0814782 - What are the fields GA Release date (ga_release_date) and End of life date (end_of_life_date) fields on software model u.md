## Issue

On the software model table (cmdb\_software\_product\_model) there are 2 OOTB field GA Release date (ga\_release\_date) and End of life date (end\_of\_life\_date). What are they used for?

## Resolution

These are legacy fields and no longer used by software asset management professional(SAMP). In SAMP our content team maintains lifecycle data for major products. When a DMAP is selected for the model the lifecycle is automatically populated.  
  
In SAMP this is how we manage lifecycle information:  
https://docs.servicenow.com/csh?topicname=software-model-fields.html&version=latest
