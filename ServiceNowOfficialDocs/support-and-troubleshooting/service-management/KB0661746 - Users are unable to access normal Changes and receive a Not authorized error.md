## Users are unable to access normal Changes and receive a "Not authorized" error

  

### Issue

Users are receiving a "Not Authorized" message when trying to access a Standard Change.  

### Cause

The Standard Change item was inactive.  

  

### Resolution

Activate the Standard Change item.
