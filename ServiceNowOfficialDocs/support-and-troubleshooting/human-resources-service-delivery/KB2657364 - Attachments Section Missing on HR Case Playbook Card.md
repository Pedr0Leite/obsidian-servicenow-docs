## Attachments Section Missing on HR Case Playbook Card

  

### Issue

Attachments section does not display on initial load of an upload task in the HR Case Playbook. The section appears only after minimizing and expanding the card. Issue observed in multiple instances and reproducible via impersonation.

### Release

Any

### Cause

·  Issue linked to platform behavior and custom configurations.

·  Not reproducible in out-of-box (OOB) instances.

### Resolution

·  Upgrade the instance to Yokohama Patch 4 or later to restore correct behavior.

·  After upgrade, attachments section displays correctly on initial load.
