## Incorrect/missing Software Publisher or Product references in Servicenow SAM content library

  

### Issue

If a Publisher or Product association  in SAM Content library is incorrect in Servicenow. Verify below before you reach out to content team for a correction.

\--> Please check the "content version" (updated in the format YYYY-MM-DD) is latest or not this would tell If a change was already performed by content is not reflected. Based on the observation you may open a content request using below KB

[https://support.servicenow.com/kb?id=kb\_article\_view&sysparm\_article=KB0790305](https://support.servicenow.com/kb?id=kb_article_view&sysparm_article=KB0790305)

\--> If change was applied , and the fields are not updated , check fields like "Stage Product , Stage publisher  or any field that you are looking to have it updated, if the change is stuck in "stage" that indicates the content has not been fully updated or the CDS jobs to update the fields have completed successfully. In this case you can Log a support case to further check the status

You can follow the same process for any incorrect content references Like PPNs, Subscription Product Definitions, DMAPs etc anything that is managed by content services team

### Release

ALL

### Resolution

Follow the Steps provided above.
