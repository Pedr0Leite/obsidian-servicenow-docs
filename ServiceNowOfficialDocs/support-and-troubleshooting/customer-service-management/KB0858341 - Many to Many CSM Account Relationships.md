## Issue

 Need the ability to create a Many-to-Many relationship between Accounts. Is there a way to do this currently? 

## Resolution

Yes its possible.  
You would need to **use `Account relationships` for horizontal** … and **`Account Hierarchy` for vertical**  
  
Read more here  [Account hierarchy](https://docs.servicenow.com/csh?topicname=c_AccountHierarchy.html&version=latest "Account hierarchy") and [Account relationship records](https://docs.servicenow.com/csh?topicname=c_AccountRelationships.html&version=latest "Account relationship records")
