## Why is the \[call\] table not extended from \[task\] table?

  

### Issue

# Description

* * *

The customer may enquire why the 'call' table (part of ServiceDesk module) is not extended from 'task' table.

# Procedure

* * *

Our engineering team decided by design not to extend above OOB table from 'task' table. Purpose of the 'call' table is to act as a scratchpad to make notes of any interaction between two or more entities. From this \[call\] record the author can decide to transfer the call to an incident, problem, change or service request.
