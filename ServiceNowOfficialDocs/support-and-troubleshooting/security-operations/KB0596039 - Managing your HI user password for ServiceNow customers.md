## Issue

Managing your HI user password for ServiceNow customers  

Overview

* * *

Customers and partners with HI accounts that log in to HI between July 14 and July 30, 2016 will be instructed to reset their password to comply with new rules for stronger passwords:

-   Passwords must be a minimum of 12 characters with 4 character types (uppercase letter, lowercase letter, number, and special character)
-   The last 10 passwords cannot be reused
-   Accounts are temporarily locked for 30 minutes after 10 unsuccessful attempts
-   Accounts are locked out after 180 days of inactivity (Contact [ServiceNow](http://www.servicenow.com/support.html "ServiceNow") to re-activate the account)
-   A HI session expires after 4 hours of inactivity

For more information, see the FAQ section in this article.

For HI password information for government customers, see [Managing your HI user password for ServiceNow customers with United States Government (USG) access](/kb_view.do?sysparm_article=KB0547663 "Managing your HI user password for ServiceNow customers with United States Government (USG) access").

Resetting your password

* * *

A user can reset their password from two locations on HI:

-   When you know your current password and can log in to HI successfully, but would like to specify a new password, navigate directly to the Change Password screen
-   When you do not know your current password or have been locked out and cannot log in to HI, use the Login screen 

A HI user password must be at least twelve characters and must contain at least one each of the following:

-   uppercase letter
-   lowercase letter
-   numeric character
-   special character (for example, !, @, #, $, (, ), %, ^, &, or \*)

You cannot reuse any of your last 10 passwords.

Changing your password

* * *

1.  Navigate to **Self-Service > Change Password**.
2.  Type your current password.
3.  Type a new password.
4.  Type the new password again.
5.  Click **Submit**.

Resetting your password from the login screen

* * *

1.  Navigate to  title="https://support.servicenow.com" target="\_blank">https://support.servicenow.com.
2.  Click **Forgot Password**.
3.  Enter your email address. 
4.  Click **Reset password**.   
    Instructions to reset your password are sent to the email address you specified.
5.  In the email message, click the reset password link.
6.  Type a new password.

Logging in after account lockout 

* * *

HI accounts are temporarily locked after 10 unsuccessful log in attempts. After the account is locked, the password reset feature cannot be used for 30 minutes. If you need immediate password assistance, [contact ServiceNow](http://www.servicenow.com/support/contact-support.html "contact ServiceNow").

Logging in after 180 days

* * *

If a ServiceNow customer does not log in to HI for 180 days, the HI account is disabled. [Contact ServiceNow](http://www.servicenow.com/support.html "contact ServiceNow") to re-activate the account.  
  
  

HI automatic session timeout  

* * *

A ServiceNow HI session expires after 4 hours of inactivity. For more information, refer to [HI Session Expiration](/kb_view.do?sysparm_article=KB0546994 "HI Session Expiration").  
  
  

FAQs  

* * *

**Q.** What if I do not remember my current password?  

A. Go to the HI login screen and click the **Forgot Password** link. An email will be sent to you with a link to reset your password, and you will not be required to input your previous password. 

**Q**. Who should I contact if I am locked out of my account?

A. If you have any concerns, please contact [Customer Support](http://www.servicenow.com/support.html "Customer Support").
