## A specific question is not getting sent to the user while triggering the Survey

  

### Issue

The user is unable to see the question on the Survey

### Cause

Question has been updated after the survey has been assigned to the user.

This will work fine if we assign this survey to a new user.

### Resolution

Complete the existing Survey for the user and re-assign the Survey to the affected user.

NOTE: You can allow the user to retake the survey by enabling re-take option on the Survey Definition.
