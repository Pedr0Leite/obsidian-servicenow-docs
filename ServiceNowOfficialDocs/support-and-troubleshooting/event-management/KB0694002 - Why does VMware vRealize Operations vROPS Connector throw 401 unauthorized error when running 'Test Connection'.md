## Issue

You are seeing a 401 unauthorized error when running 'Test Connection' on VMware vRealize Operations vROPS Connector

  

  

## Resolution

From vROPS interface, make sure that the vROPs user utilized by the vROPs connector has 'Read Access to APIs' permission granted
