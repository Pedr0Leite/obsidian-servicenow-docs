## Duplicate surveys are created during preview in designer

  

### Issue

When a survey is previewed, a duplicate copy is created in the asmt\_metric\_type list (Expected Behaviour).

The duplicate survey deletes automatically once the preview is closed. In some cases, the duplicate survey record remains as is. You can find this behaviour when a survey contains Image scale data type questions.

The issue may be affecting a single user.

### Cause

Browser cache issue

### Resolution

Clear browser cache
