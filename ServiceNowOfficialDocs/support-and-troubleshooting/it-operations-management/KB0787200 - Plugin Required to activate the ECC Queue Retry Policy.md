## Plugin Required to activate the ECC Queue Retry Policy

  

### Issue

Which Plugin needs to be activated to view the ECC Queue Retry Policy Activity below.

![](sys_attachment.do?sys_id=1ac0d8c1dbcc78d066e0a345ca961929)

### Resolution

Following Plugin needs to be activated to be able to view the ECC -> Queue Retry Policy and Queue Retry Activity modules.

Plugin Name: ECC Queue Retry Policy

ID: com.glideapp.ecc\_retry\_policy
