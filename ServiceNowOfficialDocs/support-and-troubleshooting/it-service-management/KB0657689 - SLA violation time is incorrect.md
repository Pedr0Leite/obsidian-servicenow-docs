## SLA violation time is incorrect

  

### Issue

SLA violation time is incorrect 

### Cause

SLA schedule record inactive

  

### Resolution

The schedule was deactivated for a period of time, which directly impacted SLA records. The recommended course of action is to use the Repair SLA UI Action
