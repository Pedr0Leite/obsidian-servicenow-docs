## Issue

In Flow Designer, when selecting a Catalog Item that contains variables/variable sets, the variables for the Get Catalog Variable or Create Catalog Task actions are not available to select/add.

## Resolution

Ensure all container variables on the catalog item have its 'Name' field populated with a value.
