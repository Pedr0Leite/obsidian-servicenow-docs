## Software License Compliance Checker Not Available

  

### Summary

-   It has been identified that the Software License Compliance Checker not available in version like Madrid,Newyork and Orlando but still there is a documentation which say its available for customer instance.  
    
-   It is confirmed that :
-   The "Software License Compliance Checker" is associated with the ITSM-Software Asset Management plugin --[Hyperlink](https://docs.servicenow.com/csh?topicname=c_SoftwareAssetManagement.html&version=latest#c_SoftwareAssetManagement "Hyperlink for above document")
-   However when SAM Foundation or Professional plugins are activated the " Software License Compliance Checker" functionality is no longer of relevance. Its replaced by the Reconciliation result. --[Hyperlink](https://docs.servicenow.com/csh?topicname=c_SAMReconciliationSAMF.html&version=latest "Hyperlink for above Document")
