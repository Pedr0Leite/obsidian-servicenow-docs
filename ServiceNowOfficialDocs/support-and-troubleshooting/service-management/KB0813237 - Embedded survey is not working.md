## Embedded survey is not working

  

### Issue

Unable to embed surveys in emails.

### Resolution

Surveys by default will be included in an email as a link to the actual survey. Any other form that needs to be filled will be a customised version. It needs to be scripted manually by following the instructions mentioned below.  
  
[Embed a survey within the Outlook email client](https://docs.servicenow.com/csh?topicname=embed-survey-in-outlook-email.html&version=latest)  
  
[Outlook Actionable Messages release notes](https://docs.servicenow.com/csh?topicname=store-rn-itsm-oam.html&version=latest)
