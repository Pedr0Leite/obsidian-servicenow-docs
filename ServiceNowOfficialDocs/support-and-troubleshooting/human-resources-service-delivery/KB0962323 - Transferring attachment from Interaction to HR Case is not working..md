## Transferring attachment from Interaction to HR Case is not working.

  

### Issue

Create new HR Case from Interaction doesn't transfer attachment

### Release

Paris

### Cause

Out of the box functionality

### Resolution

  
This behavior is customization not supported by ServiceNow Support.  
  
UI Action Create HR Case is responsible for transferring information from the interaction to the HR case.  
  
If you need further help with your implementation,   
Here is the link: [http://community.servicenow.com](http://community.servicenow.com "http://community.servicenow.com")
