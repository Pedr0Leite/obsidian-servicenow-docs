## Issue

-   As a Partner, you may have a need to request clones, plugin activation, access to customer instances, etc., on behalf of customers when you have a consulting agreement to do work at the customer site.
-   It is important for ServiceNow to verify that you are authorized to act on the customer's behalf.
-   Partners must provide a copy of their **Statement of Work (SOW)** signature page and details about the engagement by submitting a **Partner Deployment Request**.

## Resolution

-   Log in to the Partner Portal through the [https://partnerportal.service-now.com/partnerhome](https://partnerportal.service-now.com/partnerhome "https://partnerportal.service-now.com/partnerhome")
-   For self-registration of a Deployment, partners must create and populate the Deployment information through the Deployment Registration form.
-   Partner can follow the below steps raise a Deployment Registration Request.           
      
    **Procedure 1:**

1.  To self-register a Deployment, partners must enter information regarding the engagement in the ServiceNow Deployment Registration tool.
2.  This tool is accessed through the Partner Portal – Programs tab > Deployment Registration.

**Or  
           Procedure 2:**

1.  On the Home page itself, we now have **Deployment Registration** option. You will find it by scrolling down the page.
2.  Click on the "Create New Deployment Registration" and complete the **Deployment record** request.  
      
    PLEASE NOTE: Partner access to the customer account in HI is no longer automatically enabled when the deployment record is created. If you need to expedite access to the customer account, please contact [partnernow@servicenow.com](mailto:partnernow@servicenow.com)

When implementation work is completed, and the deployment has been registered, the parent-child correlation will be created in HI. This means that the partner will be reflected as "parent" company on the customer company record, and you, as a Partner will be able to manage your customers' request in HI through the "_Switch Company_" feature.

## Additional Information

-   If there are any issues with the deployment registration, access to customer instances or any other access that you should have after the deployment registration is completed, you can submit a Case through the "Support" module in the Partner Portal: [https://partnerportal.service-now.com/portalapp/Support.do](https://partnerportal.service-now.com/portalapp/Support.do "https://partnerportal.service-now.com/portalapp/Support.do")  
      
    
-   Should you have Partner portal access issues, please report them by sending an email to [partnernow@servicenow.com](mailto:partnernow@servicenow.com)
