## Issue

**Problem**  
The total cost calculation for license records is not updating when the unit cost is updated via transform map.

## Resolution

Review the OOB feature '[Import bulk entitlements in workspace](https://www.servicenow.com/docs/bundle/zurich-it-asset-management/page/product/software-asset-management2/task/import-entitlements-workspace.html)' or '[Import bulk entitlements in Software Asset Management classic](https://www.servicenow.com/docs/bundle/zurich-it-asset-management/page/product/software-asset-management2/task/view-entitlement-import-errors.html)' to ensure proper updates of entitlement-related fields during bulk imports.
