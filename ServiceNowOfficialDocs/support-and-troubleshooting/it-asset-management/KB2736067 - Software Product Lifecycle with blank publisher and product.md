## Software Product Lifecycle with blank publisher and product

  

### Issue

You may notice there are some sam\_sw\_product\_lifecycle records with blank Publisher and Product fields as shown in the screenshot.

These are old content files that are no longer in use. All these records are set to inactive so they are not being used. 

![](/sys_attachment.do?sys_id=a8545ea84749cf58343d8b69736d43ef "2026-01-23 17_51_11-Downloads - File Explorer.png")

### Release

All

### Resolution

They do not impact software lifecycle functions as they are set to inactive. No action is required to remove or correct them.
