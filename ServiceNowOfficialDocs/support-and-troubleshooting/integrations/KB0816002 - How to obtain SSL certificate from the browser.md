## How to obtain SSL certificate from the browser

  

### Issue

This article explains the steps to download the SSL certificate of ServiceNow from the different browsers

### Resolution

**How to Download & Export SSL Certificate the different browsers:**

**A. Internet Explorer**

1.  Open **Internet Explorer**
2.  Login to your instance
3.  Click on the top right Lock icon and click on View certificates
4.  Click on Copy to File and click on Next to download the certificate

![](sys_attachment.do?sys_id=2e0a3fa4db7b80d42be0a851ca9619ea)

**B. Firefox:**

1.  Open **Firefox**
2.  Login to your instance
3.  Click on the top left Lock icon and click on More Information
4.  Select Security tab and click on View Certificate
5.  Select Details tab and click on Export

![](sys_attachment.do?sys_id=62ee7b68dbbb80d42be0a851ca9619f1) 

**C. Chrome**

1.  Open **Chrome**
2.  Login to your instance
3.  Click on the top left Lock icon and then click Certificate
4.  Select Details tab and click on Copy to file...
5.  Click on Next and select the right format of the certificate to export
