## Issue

Layer 2 Discovered device relations can be made visible in the Dependency View by selecting the dependency type Physical Network Connections. However, they are NOT visible under CI Relations Formatter on the CI form.

## Resolution

1) In order to view the connection, you must navigate to the dependency view of the switch, Select the dependency type as Physical Network Connections and then only you would be able to view the layer 2 connection between the switch and the server.
