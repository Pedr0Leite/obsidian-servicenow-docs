## HR onboarding language support

  

### Summary

For HR onboarding and transitions,which all of the below languages are supported? EN - ES - DE - CN (trad) - CN (simpl) - FR - IT - HI - CZ - PT - SE - PL - JP - KR - DU - FI - TR - MY - TH - DK - RU

### Instructions

Any languages supported by the ServiceNow platform should also be supported for HR onboarding and transitions, there isn't anything special about onboarding and transitions.
