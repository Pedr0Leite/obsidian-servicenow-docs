## Issue

Journey Designer - when pressing 'Add Mentor' the department of the Mentor is showing another employees department. However, when looking back end the department is correct.

## Resolution

To resolve this issue, follow these steps:  
  
1\. Go to the HR Profile for the user  
2\. Set the Position to one with the correct Department
