## How to identify your Now Support admin

  

### Issue

Each ServiceNow customer designates one or more employees to serve as Now Support administrators. These admins have specific privileges, [roles and responsibilities](https://support.servicenow.com/kb?id=kb_article_view&sysparm_article=KB0538359) within Now Support, which enables them to:

-   [Create and manage user records](https://support.servicenow.com/kb?id=kb_article_view&sysparm_article=KB1284666) for individuals in their organizations.
-   [Reset passwords](https://support.servicenow.com/kb?id=kb_article_view&sysparm_article=KB0547255) for users.

Before contacting ServiceNow Technical Support for issues related to Now Support accounts, contact your organization's Now Support admin. They have the necessary privileges and access to address most account-related issues efficiently.

### Resolution

### Identify your Now Support admin

After you log in to Now Support, follow these steps to identify your Now Support admin:

1.  Go to the [Now Support portal.](/now "Now Support portal")
2.  In the upper right corner, select your user name, and then select on **My Profile**.
3.  See who is listed in the **ServiceNow admins** table for account support.
