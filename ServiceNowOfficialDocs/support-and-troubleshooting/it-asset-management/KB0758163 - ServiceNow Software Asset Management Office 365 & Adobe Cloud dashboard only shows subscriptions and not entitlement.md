## Issue

After installing the com.snc.samp.microsoft plugin you are unable to see entitlements from Microsoft Office 365 portal in the ServiceNow "Office 365 & Adobe Cloud" dashboard.

## Resolution

At this time the ServiceNow Software Asset Management "Office 365 & Adobe Cloud" dashboard only shows subscriptions and not entitlement.
