## Issue

Unable to create Direct Integration Profile from 'Software Asset Workspace' even AFTER repairing 'sn\_sam\_saas\_int' Plugin as detailed in [KB1641765](https://support.servicenow.com/kb?id=kb_article_view&sysparm_article=KB1641765)

## Resolution

'Repair' of the 'Software Asset Management Professional Core' \[com.snc.samp.core\] Plugin seems to have resolved the issue.
