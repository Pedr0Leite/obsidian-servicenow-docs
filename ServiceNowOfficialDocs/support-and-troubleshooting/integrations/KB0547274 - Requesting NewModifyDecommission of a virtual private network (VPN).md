## Requesting New/Modify/Decommission of a virtual private network (VPN)

  

### Issue

For all virtual private network (VPN) requests, including provisioning, modifications, decommissioning or general questions, use the Service Catalog VPN Requests form.

For VPN details, see [Virtual Private Network (VPN)](https://docs.servicenow.com/en-US/bundle/vancouver-platform-security/page/administer/encryption/concept/c_SetUpAVPN4SNowBusNet.html "Virtual Private Network (VPN)") in the product documentation. 

### Submitting VPN requests

1.  Navigate to [Now Support](https://support.servicenow.com/now "Now Support").
2.  Click **Automation Store**.
3.  Under **Cloud Infrastructure**, click ****VPN Requests.****
4.  Select the appropriate VPN request type: **New VPN provisioning** or **Modify existing VPN** or **Decommission VPN Tunnels**:  
      
    ![](/sys_attachment.do?sys_id=37d1e4c64774b990da8f9535126d438a)  
      
    
5.  Answer the questions.  
    Questions vary depending on the request type selected. In some fields, you can click **More Information** for additional details about the field.
6.  Click **Submit**.
