## Unable to view roles in Contact Roles / contact-role-slushbucket widget

  

### Issue

Unable to view roles in Contact Roles / contact-role-slushbucket widget 

### Release

All

### Cause

Roles not updated in property 'sn\_customerservice.contact\_role\_assignment'

### Resolution

Add the additional roles which are not available in the 'sn\_customerservice.contact\_role\_assignment' property to resolved the issue.
