## How to install new Discovery and Service Mapping Patterns from the ServiceNow Store

  

### Issue

Install new Discovery and Service Mapping patterns from the ServiceNow Store to automatically discover and map IT infrastructure in your ServiceNow environment.

  

### Release

  All supported releases

### Resolution

####  Installation steps

1.  Go to store.servicenow.com
2.  In the search field, type **Discovery and Service Mapping Patterns.**
3.  From the search results, select **Discovery and Service Mapping Patterns** by ServiceNow**.**
4.  Select **Request Install**.
    -   Enter your instance name (format: instancename.service-now.com).
    -   Enter the business reason for the request.
5.  Review your request in the request history section. 
6.  Access your ServiceNow instance.
7.  In the Application Navigator, go to **System Applications** > **Applications.** 
8.  In the applications list, locate **Discovery and Service Mapping Patterns** and select **Install**.
9.  Verify the installation
    -   Go to **Discovery** \> **Discovery Patterns** in the Application Navigator
    -   Confirm the new patterns appear in the Discovery Pattern table.
